Our Process

How We Work

We are quick to respond to any query. Just phone us, and we’ll get back to you straight away! If you’re searching for custom cabinet makers near me, just call us!

When organising a meeting, we always select the day and time that works best for the customer. You can count on us to be there on time.

1
Step 01Free Consultation

Meeting the Customer

We offer an onsite consultation with the customer. Once we have discussed their needs, we take the necessary measurements and advise them on their cabinet options. Experience the joy of creation without financial constraints – consultation, measurement, and design come free!

2
Step 02Within 1–3 Days

Rough Estimation

Shortly after taking measurements, we provide a rough estimate, usually within 1-3 days. This initial step gives you a general idea of the project cost, ensuring transparency right from the start.

3
Step 03Your Approval

Detailed Design Process

Upon your approval of the estimate, we move to the detailed design phase. Our team crafts a comprehensive design and forwards it for your approval. We welcome and incorporate your feedback, making as many changes as necessary until the design aligns perfectly with your vision.

4
Step 04Exact Cost

Precise Quotation

Once you approve the design, we provide an exact quotation. This step ensures you know the precise cost involved before we commence production. Our quotations are detailed, transparent, and tailored to your specific project requirements.

5
Step 05Quality Materials

Commencing Production

With your satisfaction on the quote, production begins. We use quality materials and employ expert craftsmanship to turn the approved design into reality. Our focus remains on maintaining the highest standards throughout the production process.

6
Step 06Hassle-Free

Scheduling Installation

Two weeks before completing production, we contact you to schedule the installation day. Our professional team arrives on the chosen date, delivering and expertly installing your custom product. We ensure a hassle-free installation, respecting your time and space.

Flexible Payment Options

We require a 50% deposit before production begins. The balance is due upon job completion, offering you two convenient options:

Option 1Pay the balance within 7 days post-completion.
Option 2Opt for instalment payments up to 12 months, with equal amounts due every 2 weeks.

Our payment terms are designed to offer flexibility and ease, aligning with your financial convenience.

Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Everything you need to know about our custom cabinet design and installation services.

Still have questions?Call us on +61 4XX XXX XXX – we're happy to help.
How much does a custom cabinet cost?
The cost depends on the size of the space, materials, finishes and hardware you choose. After a free consultation and measure, we provide a precise, itemised quote with no hidden costs.
How long does it take from design to installation?
Most projects take a few weeks from design approval to installation, depending on the scope and materials. We'll give you a clear timeline at the quotation stage and keep you updated throughout.
Do you offer free design consultations and quotes?
Yes. We offer a design consultation to understand your needs and measure your space, followed by a detailed design proposal and quotation.
What materials and finishes do you use?
We use premium, durable materials and offer a wide range of modern finishes – including matte, gloss, timber-look and textured options – plus quality soft-close hardware.
Which rooms do you build cabinets for?
Kitchens and pantries, wardrobes and walk-in robes, laundries, bathrooms, garages, home offices, TV and entertainment units, and more.
Are you insured?
Yes – Fit My House is fully insured by AAMI for $5,000,000 AUD, giving you complete peace of mind on every project.
Do you offer flexible payment options?
Yes. We offer staged payments aligned with your project's progress – a deposit to confirm, a progress payment during production and the balance after installation.
