Why go solar

Benefits of Solar Installation in Wagga Wagga

Solar can make sense for many Wagga Wagga homes and businesses. Here is what a well-designed system can offer.

Lower energy bills

Generating your own power during the day can reduce how much electricity you buy from the grid. Savings depend on your system size, usage and tariff.

Sustainability

Cleaner power that reduces your reliance on fossil fuels and lowers your carbon footprint.

25years

Product warranty, plus 5 years on workmanship

Rebates

More energy independence

Add a solar battery to use more of your own power and rely less on the grid.

Increased property value

Many buyers see an installed solar system as an attractive feature when comparing homes.

Savings and incentives vary by property and change over time. Ask us for a quote based on your own bills.
