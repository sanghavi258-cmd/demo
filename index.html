Registration Journey

NDIS Registration Process Step by Step

Follow eight stages from identifying registration groups to receiving a Certificate of Registration if approved.

STEP 1

Identify Groups

Identify the registration groups that match the supports your organisation proposes to deliver to NDIS participants.

STEP 2

Prepare Application

Prepare the NDIS Commission application with organisation details, key personnel, applicable self-assessment responses, policies and evidence.

STEP 3

Submit Application

Submit the application through the NDIS Commission portal once all your details and evidence are complete.

STEP 4

Receive Scope

Receive the Initial Scope of Audit from the Commission, which confirms the type of audit required.

STEP 5

Engage Auditor

Select and engage an independent Approved Quality Auditor of your choice from the Commission's approved list.

STEP 6

Complete Audit

Complete the NDIS quality audit required by your Initial Scope of Audit, either verification or certification.

STEP 7

Commission Review

Respond to any Commission requests for information while it reviews your application, audit recommendation and suitability.

STEP 8

Receive Certificate

Receive your Certificate of Registration if approved, showing your registration scope, period and any applicable conditions.

Practice Standards

NDIS Practice Standards Audit: Verification and Certification

Audit requirements depend on your registration scope, so confirm rules on the NDIS Commission website.

Desktop Review

Verification Audit

Verification generally applies to lower-risk or lower-complexity supports and services and involves a desktop review of the required documentary evidence.

Stage 1 + Stage 2

Certification Audit

Certification generally applies to higher-risk or more complex supports and services and includes a Stage 1 desktop audit and Stage 2 onsite audit.

Initial Scope

Your Initial Scope of Audit confirms if an NDIS certification audit or a verification audit applies.

Auditor Selection

You choose and engage the Approved Quality Auditor, who remains independent of you and Amal Consulting.

Processing Times

Processing times vary, and the Commission decides each application after reviewing the audit and your suitability.
