Home batteries

Benefits of Adding a Solar Battery

Most Melbourne homes send their spare solar to the grid for a small feed-in credit, then buy power back at night. A home battery keeps that energy for you, so more of your solar is used where it counts.

Lower power bills

Use your own stored solar instead of paying peak rates, and cut the amount you buy from your retailer each quarter.

Backup during outages

With backup-capable systems, lights, fridge and Wi-Fi keep running when the grid goes down in a storm.

Use stored solar at night

Charge the battery from your panels during the day and power cooking, heating and TV after sunset.

Less reliance on the grid

Take more control of your energy and protect your household from future electricity price rises.

How much does solar cost in Melbourne?

The cost of solar depends on system size, panel and inverter brands, your roof type and any rebates you're eligible for. A typical home system is sized to your energy use and roof space. We provide a free, itemised quote after reviewing your bills, so you know exactly what you're paying for and the savings to expect.

Is a solar battery worth it?

A battery is usually worth it if you export a lot of solar during the day and use most of your power in the evening. It lets you use your own stored energy instead of buying from the grid, and can provide backup during outages. With current rebates, payback times have improved. We'll model the savings for your household.

How long does installation take?

Most residential solar installations are completed in a single day, while larger homes or solar-plus-battery systems may take one to two days. Before that, network approvals typically take a few weeks. We'll give you a clear timeline at the quote stage and keep you updated from approval through to switch-on.

What rebates can I get?

Eligible households may access the federal Cheaper Home Batteries Program, Small-scale Technology Certificates (STCs) for solar, and Victorian programs through Solar Victoria. Eligibility depends on your property, income and existing system. As an accredited installer, we check what applies to you and include eligible incentives in your quote.

Can I add a battery to my existing solar?

Yes. Most existing solar systems can have a battery added, either with an AC-coupled battery or by upgrading to a hybrid inverter. We'll inspect your current inverter, panels and switchboard, then recommend the most cost-effective battery option to get the most from the solar you already have.

What warranties do you offer?

Your system is covered by manufacturer warranties — typically 25 years on panel performance, 5–10 years on inverters and around 10 years on batteries — plus our own workmanship warranty on the installation. If an issue arises, we manage the claim for you so you're not left dealing with manufacturers alone.

Do you install for businesses?

Yes. We design and install commercial solar and battery systems for offices, warehouses, retail, farms and other businesses. Commercial solar can significantly reduce daytime operating costs. We'll assess your energy profile and roof, then provide a tailored proposal with expected savings and payback.

Which areas do you service?

We're based in Berwick and install solar panels and batteries across Melbourne, with a focus on the south-east — including Pakenham, Narre Warren, Cranbourne, Officer, Dandenong and surrounding suburbs. Not sure if we cover your area? Give us a call and we'll let you know.
