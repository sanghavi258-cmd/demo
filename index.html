Understanding STA

What is NDIS Short Term Accommodation?

NDIS Short Term Accommodation, previously known as respite, is a funded support allowing participants to stay somewhere away from home for a short period while continuing to receive the care and assistance they need.

STA is suitable for participants who want a change of environment, need temporary care arrangements, or are working toward greater independence in a supported setting. It also provides planned relief for families and carers.

STA funding is generally drawn from Core Supports under Assistance with Daily Life, and most participants can access up to 28 days per year depending on their plan and individual circumstances.

What's Included

What NDIS Short Term Accommodation Covers

NDIS STA is designed to support participant wellbeing, build independence, and provide planned respite for families and carers. A fully supported STA stay typically includes:

Accessible accommodation suited to individual needs

Personal care assistance including hygiene, dressing, and mobility

Nutritious meals prepared with dietary requirements in mind

Overnight supervision and on-call support as required

Assistance with daily living routines and tasks

Community access, social activities, and supported outings during the stay

What STA does not cover

STA is not intended as permanent housing, long-term crisis accommodation, or hospital-level care. All supports must align with your NDIS goals and meet the reasonable and necessary criteria set by the NDIS.

Know the Difference

STA and Respite Care: Understanding the Difference

Short Term Accommodation (STA) is a specific type of NDIS support. Respite care is a broader term for support that gives unpaid carers a break from their caring responsibilities.

Funding Explained

NDIS Funding for Short Term Accommodation

STA funding is provided under Core Supports: Assistance with Daily Life in your NDIS plan. Most participants can access up to 28 days per year of STA, used flexibly as single nights, weekends, or multiple short-term stays depending on individual needs and plan approval.

STA funding is available to self-managed, plan-managed, and NDIA-managed participants. All services must align with the NDIS Price Guide and meet the reasonable and necessary criteria set by the NDIS.

What household tasks can NDIS funding cover in Melbourne?

NDIS funding can cover a range of household tasks including cleaning, laundry, meal preparation, and general domestic assistance, provided these supports align with your NDIS goals and meet the reasonable and necessary criteria in your plan.

Who is eligible for NDIS assistance with household tasks?

NDIS participants whose disability directly affects their ability to manage everyday household tasks may be eligible for household task assistance. Eligibility depends on your individual plan, goals, and functional capacity assessment outcomes.

Does NDIS household support include cleaning, laundry, and meal preparation?

Yes. NDIS household task support can include general cleaning, laundry, ironing, meal preparation, and other domestic activities. The specific tasks covered depend on what is included in your individual NDIS plan and support goals.

How can I check if my NDIS plan covers household task assistance?

Review your plan under Core Supports: Assistance with Daily Life, or speak with your support coordinator or plan manager. Our team can also help you understand what household task supports may be available within your current funding.

How can I arrange NDIS household task support in Melbourne?

Simply contact Peace of Mind directly by phone or email. Our team will discuss your support needs, review your funding, and help arrange appropriate household task assistance as part of your broader NDIS support plan.

How long can I stay in NDIS short term accommodation?

Most participants can access up to 28 days per year of STA, depending on their NDIS plan and individual goals. Stays can be used as single nights, weekends, or longer arrangements based on your circumstances.

Can families stay in contact during an STA stay?

Yes. Peace of Mind encourages open communication between participants, families, and our support team throughout every stay. Families are kept informed and can contact our team at any time during the participant’s stay.
