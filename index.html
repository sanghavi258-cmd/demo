Childcare & Kindergarten in Truganina

Little Learners is a daycare in Truganina for children aged 6 months to 6 years. Our early learning childcare centre runs play-based programs that build social, language, number and movement skills, indoors and outdoors. Educators attend to each child’s individual needs and keep families informed about their day. Families looking for childcare in Truganina can visit the centre, meet the team and see the rooms before enrolling.

Kindergarten and Early Learning

Kindergarten Program for 3 and 4 Year Olds

Our Government-approved kindergarten program is open to 3 and 4 year olds. Children take part in early literacy, numeracy and social skills activities that build confidence for primary school. As an early learning childcare centre, we run the kinder program alongside our childcare program, so your child can stay in one centre as they grow.
