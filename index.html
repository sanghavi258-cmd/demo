What NDIS disability support services does Double Bay Disability Care provide?

We provide Supported Independent Living (SIL), Specialised Disability Accommodation (SDA), assistance with personal care, high intensity personal care, household tasks, community participation, innovative community participation, travel and transport, therapeutic support, community nursing, PEG feeding support and dementia care.

Is Double Bay Disability Care a registered NDIS provider?

Yes. Double Bay Disability Care is a registered NDIS provider, delivering services in line with NDIS quality and safety standards.

Does Double Bay Disability Care offer Supported Independent Living?

Yes. Our SIL services provide help and supervision with daily tasks in a shared or individual home, so you can live as independently as possible.

What personal care and daily living support services are available?

We help with personal hygiene, showering, dressing, grooming, meal preparation, cleaning, laundry and daily routines, as well as high intensity personal care for more complex needs.

Does Double Bay Disability Care provide community nursing services?

Yes. We offer community nursing delivered in the home and community, including complex care and PEG feeding support.

Does Double Bay Disability Care offer community participation support?

Yes. We support participants to take part in social, recreational and community activities, including innovative community participation programs and travel and transport assistance.

Does Double Bay Disability Care provide high intensity personal care?

Yes. Our trained team provides high intensity personal care for participants with complex support needs that require a higher level of skill.
