FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

How long does a solar installation take?

Most residential installs are completed in a single day. We'll handle approvals and grid connection paperwork before and after the install.

Do I need a battery with my solar system?

Not always. A battery lets you store excess daytime energy for use at night — we'll show you whether it pays off based on your usage.

What rebates am I eligible for?

Eligibility depends on your location and circumstances. Our team checks every available incentive and handles the paperwork for you.

What warranties do you offer?

Panels typically carry 25-year performance warranties, plus manufacturer warranties on inverters and batteries and our own workmanship warranty.

How does the Refer & Earn program work?

Refer someone using our form; once their system is installed and paid for, we send you your reward. There's no limit on referrals.
